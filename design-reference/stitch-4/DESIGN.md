## Brand & Style

The design system projects the presence of an elite, visionary creative digital studio. It balances the timeless authority of an avant-garde editorial publication with the kinetic technical precision of modern software craft. The audience encompasses venture-backed founders, luxury brands, cultural institutions, and forward-thinking enterprises seeking category-defining digital flagship experiences.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Editorial Brutalism meets Haute-Digital Craft:** The aesthetic fuses stark, deliberate architectural grids with bespoke editorial sensitivity. It draws inspiration from high-concept studios (such as Koto, Pentagram, and Instrument), using high-contrast typography, hairline dividing rules, dynamic asymmetry, and tactile organic materiality.
- **Physicality and Surface Integrity:** Interfaces feel tangible rather than synthetic. Crisp warm white sheet surfaces sit gracefully atop an archival warm cream bedrock, complemented by micro-grain textures, simulated browser frame chrome, and precisely weighted structural lines.
- **Tone and Voice:** Confident, culturally acute, razor-sharp, and unapologetically refined. Zero decorative filler; every pixel, rule, and baseline lockup serves deliberate narrative intent.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate high-end tactile atmosphere. It abandons sterile synthetic whites in favor of museum-grade cream substrates, paired with deep maritime ink and punctuated by sudden bursts of electric cobalt energy.

### Core Swatches & Roles
- **Canvas Substrate (`neutral_color_hex` / `#F6F0E6`):** Warm cream. The foundational atmosphere across pages, providing a grounded, paper-like baseline.
- **Dominant Structural & Primary Ink (`primary_color_hex` / `#17243A`):** Deep navy. Applied to major display titles, heavy button treatments, structural boundaries, and dark container backgrounds.
- **Kinetic Accent (`secondary_color_hex` / `#315BFF`):** Electric cobalt blue. Used sparingly for interactive highlights, dynamic focus rings, live status tags, call-to-action fills, and primary kinetic signals.
- **Warm Editorial Accent (`tertiary_color_hex` / `#F2B9A0`):** Soft peach. Used for highlighting editorial callouts, case study tags, and warm tonal backgrounds.
- **Atmospheric Tint (`#DCD9F5`):** Pale lavender. Provides a cool, whimsical counterpoint to peach within categorization tags, hover glows, and visual badges.
- **Elevated Canvas Surface (`#FFFDF9`):** Crisp warm white. The surface color for floating browser cards, interactive cards, and module sheets.
- **Micro-Contrast Ink (`#202020`):** Dark ink. Strictly calibrated for high-legibility body prose, technical tabular figures, and fine hairlines.

### Color Distribution Rules
Maintain an 80-15-5 hierarchy: 80% warm cream substrate and warm white surfaces, 15% deep navy and dark ink typographic hierarchy, and 5% surgical electric cobalt blue energy with peach/lavender accents. Avoid large swaths of cobalt; it commands full attention through strict restraint.

## Typography

Typography embodies the tension between sculptural expression and engineering precision.

### Type Pairings & Editorial Rhythm
- **Display & Headers (Syne):** Sculptural, avant-garde, and confident. Set tight with negative letter-spacing for headlines to create an unmistakable silhouette on the page. Used for hero titles, section milestones, large numbers, and statement quotes.
- **Prose & Interface (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern, geometric, clear, and perfectly balanced. Neutral enough to support extensive body reading, but endowed with subtly geometric curves that harmonize with Syne's architectural weight.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Micro-labels utilize uppercase transformations with expanded letter tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.05em` to `0.08em`) to mimic architectural blueprint legends, project specs, and editorial magazine marginalia.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy champions disciplined asymmetric grids and architectural structure.

### Grid & Compositional Rhythm
- **12-Column Asymmetric Canvas (Desktop > 1024px):** A flexible 12-column grid anchored by substantial outer borders (`margin: 3.5rem`). Sections feature unaligned, conversational layouts—such as a 5-column case study statement paired across a 1-column void with a 6-column interactive showcase window.
- **8-Column Fluid Canvas (Tablet 768px – 1023px):** Compacted gutter architecture with full-bleed container sections to maintain visual grandeur.
- **4-Column Direct Flow (Mobile < 768px):** Tight, high-density vertical layout where editorial headers break into stacked lines, maximizing typographic scale over blank horizontal space.

### Structural Lines & Dividers
Grid lines are deliberately surfaced rather than hidden. Use 1px hairline borders (`rgba(23, 36, 58, 0.12)`) as explicit horizontal baseline rules and vertical track demarcations to section off projects, meta-data sidebars, and client rosters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through structural layering, crisp hairline containment, and sharp atmospheric depth rather than heavy soft drops.

### Tactile Physical Layers
- **Substrate Base (Level 0):** The raw Warm Cream (`#F6F0E6`) surface infused with a persistent SVG noise grain overlay (3% opacity, blend mode `multiply`).
- **Sheet Surfaces (Level 1):** Warm White (`#FFFDF9`) cards framed with a crisp, low-contrast 1px border (`#17243A` at 12% opacity). Elevation is rendered via an ultra-subtle directional wash: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(23, 36, 58, 0.06)`.
- **Floating Artifacts & Windows (Level 2):** Elevated mockup windows and browser chrome cards feature a precise dual-stage shadow:
  - Ambient base: `0 2px 4px rgba(23, 36, 58, 0.04)`
  - Key light: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(23, 36, 58, 0.10)`
- **Interactive Focus & Hover States:** Active cards do not merely float; they shift smoothly on hover (`translateY(-4px)`), while their 1px hairline border transitions from muted slate ink to Electric Cobalt Blue (`#315BFF`) or Deep Navy (`#17243A`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes architectural precision. Form elements strike a sharp balance: structurally confident cards meet organic pill geometries.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Panels, Cards, and Browser Containers:** Built with consistent `0.5rem` (8px) corners, preserving crisp rectangular tension without harsh 90-degree points.
- **Browser Window Chrome:** Top corners conform to `0.5rem`, holding authentic browser control dots (three uniform 8px circles with 6px spacing) and crisp horizontal tab silhouettes.
- **Pill Badges & Buttons:** Expressive controls, client discipline badges, and category chips utilize a total radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px), producing immediate tactile contrast against rectilinear grids and case study preview viewports.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Navy (`#17243A`) background with warm cream text (`#F6F0E6`), rounded pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.875rem 1.75rem`. On hover, transitions effortlessly to Electric Cobalt Blue (`#315BFF`) with crisp foreground stability.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent fill, defined by a 1.5px Deep Navy border. Text in Deep Navy. On hover, fills with pale lavender (`#DCD9F5`) while retaining text color.
- **Kinetic Studio Action (Magnetic Button):** Circular or oversized pill button featuring an arrow indicator that pivots 45 degrees upon cursor focus, enclosed in an Electric Cobalt micro-border.

### Studio Browser Windows & Mockups
- Dedicated custom frames displaying studio output. Features an authentic top chrome header: warm white surface, three hairline dots (representing window controls) in muted ink, an optional centered pill URL bar with monospaced typography, and a seamless media container directly below.
- Outlined with a 1px solid hairline (`rgba(23, 36, 58, 0.12)`) and elevated with Level 2 studio depth.

### Pill Badges & Metadata Tags
- **Category & Discipline Badges:** Compact pill elements (`rounded-full`) utilizing `0.25rem 0.75rem` padding.
- **Color Variations:** Soft Peach (`#F2B9A0`) background with Deep Navy typography; Pale Lavender (`#DCD9F5`) background with Deep Navy typography; or transparent cream with 1px border.
- **Typography:** `label-sm` or `label-md` uppercase, tracked, indicating services like "CREATIVE DIRECTION", "WEBGL DEV", or "STRATEGY".

### Lists & Client Rosters
- Set as full-width index tables divided by 1px hairlines (`rgba(23, 36, 58, 0.12)`).
- Rows expand slightly on hover (`space-md` to `space-lg` visual breathing room), triggering an instant cursor-following project image preview thumbnail.
- Uses tabular numbering (`01`, `02`, `03`) set in `label-md` aligned to the extreme left.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Clean architectural input fields. Background is Warm White (`#FFFDF9`) with a 1px solid border in muted ink (`rgba(23, 36, 58, 0.2)`).
- Text styled in `body-md` (`#202020`). Focus state transitions the border to Electric Cobalt Blue (`#315BFF`) with zero generic glow rings—maintaining a clean, graphic 1px hairline shift.
- Floating or uppercase pinned labels rendered in `label-sm` Deep Navy.

### Interactive Case Study Cards
- Multi-layered card system: Warm White canvas base, inset media area with an exact 4:3 or 16:10 aspect ratio, followed by project meta-information (Title in `headline-sm`, Year and Category in `label-md`).
- Bottom row features pill tags and a sliding interactive CTA that reveals an accented arrow on cursor enter.
