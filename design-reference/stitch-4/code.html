Questions & Answers

Everything clearly answered.

No vague policies. Here is exactly how we deliver each project.

Are there any recurring or hidden studio fees?

expand_more

None. The price quoted on this page is the exact flat one-time design and development cost. You never receive unannounced maintenance invoices or surprise deployment surcharges.

How do domain registration and web hosting work?

expand_more

For microsites (Invitations & Birthday pages), we provide zero-cost hosted links. For portfolios and business websites, we guide you directly to purchase your domain (.com, .in) under your own name (costing approximately ₹600–₹900/year via GoDaddy or Namecheap) and link it to high-speed cloud hosting (often free on Vercel/Netlify or minimal shared servers). You retain 100% legal ownership of your digital assets.

How many revisions are included in each project?

expand_more

Each tier includes structured revision rounds (1 round for micro-sites, 2 rounds for portfolios & small business, and 3 rounds for flagship business & e-commerce builds). Revisions cover copy tweaks, layout adjustments, color refinement, and media substitutions to ensure complete satisfaction before the public link goes live.

What is the exact process from payment to launch?

expand_more

1. Tap "Start a Project" to transmit your inquiry via WhatsApp or Email.
2. Complete a streamlined 5-minute brief detailing your text, images, and preferences.
3. We build your initial preview prototype within the specified timeframe (2 to 14 days depending on tier).
4. Review, request revisions, and launch to your live URL.
